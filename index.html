🍏 macOS Installation Guide

1. Click the "Download for macOS" button above to get the .dmg file.
2. Open the downloaded Flower_Power-v1.0.3-macos.dmg file.
3. Simply drag and drop the Flower Power icon into your Applications folder.
4. Open the app from your Launchpad!

Note: Because this is an open-source app downloaded outside the Mac App Store, your Mac might show a security warning. To bypass it, simply right-click the Flower Power app icon and select 'Open'.

🪟 Windows Installation Guide

1. Click the "Download for Windows" button above to get the .exe installer.
2. Double-click the downloaded Flower_Power-v1.0.3-windows.exe file.
3. Follow the simple installation wizard instructions on your screen.
4. Launch Flower Power from your Desktop shortcut or Start Menu!

Note: If Windows SmartScreen blocks the installation, click "More Info" and then "Run anyway".
